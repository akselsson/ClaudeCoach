# Season plan — PRT 100 km, Dec 4 2026

## Context

- **Base:** post-Mauerweglauf (163 km, Aug 15) rebuild. Weeks Aug 24 → Sep 21: 34, 17, 43, 54, 44 km. Longest run before today was 23 km (Sep 11). Quality back for three weeks: Sep 4 hard 17 km, Sep 11 long with threshold, Sep 17–18 threshold days. Kundo has resumed.
- **Last year's PRT build:** Oct 6 → Nov 9 2025 ran 65, 91, 94, 79, 101 km/week, then almost nothing Nov 10–23. **That gap was the shin problem starting**, not a taper. The shins now show mainly under high mileage in unstable shoes (Boston 13, Evo SL).
- **2025 PRT 12h, activity 16658939467:** 115.3 km official (Strava 117.9, +2% over-read), avg HR 145, max 157, 2nd place. Per-10-km GPS pace and HR: 6:19/142, 6:00/144, 6:09/149, **5:36/149, 5:33/152, 5:41/151**, 6:05/150, 6:05/147, 6:32/142, 6:19/141, 6:40/140, 6:13/140. Real 100 km ≈ 10:20. ## Goals

| | Time | Pace, real km | What it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Floor** | **sub-10:00** | 6:00/km | Last year's first-80-km pace, held to the end. A fuelling and format fix on last year's fitness. |
| **Stretch** | **sub-9:00** | 5:24/km | ~3% faster than last year's best 30 km stretch, sustained three times as long. Needs a real economy step at 100 km effort, not just a cleaner race. |

**The pacing decision is deferred to the race plan** (~Nov 20). Patrik's own rule: if sub-9 looks attainable, it is sub-9 from the start. To make that a data decision rather than a mood decision, the peak block contains a gate:

> **Sub-9 gate:** a flat 30 km at avg HR ≤148, in the intended race shoe, holding ≤5:25/km GPS (≈5:30 real). Scheduled as the closing segment of the week-6 long run, Nov 13. Pass → the race plan is written for sub-9 with sub-10 as the safety net. Miss by >10 s/km → paced for a certain sub-10 with sub-9 only if the day allows.

The 100 km format removes last year's motivational problem: the fixed-time race had no finish line to run toward in the last 20 km. This one does. The Lidingöloppet lesson still applies at km 85 of a bad day — the race plan will carry a fallback target that survives a bad patch.

## Weekly shape

Unchanged from preferences: **Mon off · Tue Kundo + heavy strength · Wed off · Thu medium-long + maintenance strength · Fri long (the anchor) · Sat/Sun daughter run one day, flex the other.** B2B in week 6 = Friday long + daughter run Saturday.

## Training style: polarized, with the race-specific work inside the long run

Same framework as the Berlin build and for the same reasons (injury risk, matches the Tue + Fri rhythm). What changes for a fast flat 100 km:

- **The Friday long carries a closing segment at 100 km effort** — HR 145–150, pace wherever that lands (expect 5:20–5:45 on the flat, faster as the block progresses). 30 min in week 2, growing to 60–90 min by the peak. This is the specific stimulus for sub-9 and the only place it needs to live.
- **Tuesday stays Kundo's call.** Threshold work from the group is welcome; VO2 two weeks running gets the second taken at threshold, as the preference file already says.
- **Everything else is HR <140–145.** Thursday medium-long included, apart from strides.
- **No hill blocks, no track.** That is next summer's note (see the Lidingöloppet review), not this build's.

## Fuelling — the principle, rehearsed in training

Last year: **90 g/h from 3 scoops Umara in 500 ml — an 18% solution.** Nausea at km 70–80, rescued by solids, water and cola, never back on sports drink. The carb *total* was right; the *delivery* was the problem. Hypertonic drink sits in the stomach, and the deficit it creates is the same fluid-then-bolus chain the Mauerweglauf review documented.

Principle for Dec 4, to be turned into a per-hour card in the race plan:

- **Keep 90 g/h.** Do not cut carbs; that is what cost pace after the dip last year.
- **Dilute:** the drink at 6–8% (≈ 60 g in 750–1000 ml), which covers fluid needs at the same time.
- **The remaining 30 g/h solid or gel from hour one**, so the gut is trained on mixed sources before it is stressed, not after.
- **Cola from roughly hour five** as a planned switch, not a rescue. It worked last year and in Berlin.
- **The Mauerweglauf escalation ladder is race-proven** (`../races/2026-08-16-mauerweglauf-race-review.md`): taste → reluctance → sloshing → burping → nausea, act at rung 1–2 by cutting concentration, not intake.

**Rehearsal:** every Friday long from week 3 runs the race mix at 60–90 g/h. The week-6 and week-7 long runs are full dress rehearsals with the table set up as on race day. A 350 m loop with an own table means per-lap access; the card will be written per hour and the table stocked per hour.

## Shoes — the injury-prevention plan, not a gear note

| Role | Shoes | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Volume, easy and medium-long | **Altra Torin 8, Superblast 3, Megablast** | These carry the 80–110 km weeks. Rotate; no shoe two days running when the weekly total is above 90. |
| Short and treadmill only | Boston 13, Evo SL (both pairs) | **≤10 km per outing, never on the long run.** This is the shin rule. |
| Trail variety | Lone Peak 9+ | Weekend daylight only; darkness makes evening trail impractical. Counts as easy volume. |
| Race-effort segments and race | Metaspeed Sky Paris, larger Evo SL, possibly Altra Vanish Carbon 3 | See below. |

**Race shoe is open**, three candidates. Last year: started in the too-small Evo SL, switched to the Metaspeed. This year's idea is the reverse, with the larger Evo SL. The Vanish Carbon 3 is an un-bought option.

- **Any shoe that starts on Dec 4 has run at least three Friday long runs with race-effort segments before Nov 13**, including the sub-9 gate run. A shoe that has not done that is not a race shoe, however good the chart says it is.
- The Evo SL shin rule does not apply to race-effort segments of 30–90 min in the *larger* pair — but it does mean the Evo SL is not the shoe for the first 60 km of a 100 km. If it is used at all on race day, it is the back-half shoe, when the segment is short.
- **Check the race's stack-height rule** before deciding: Patrik's understanding is that 40 mm+ shoes are banned, which excludes the Superblast and Megablast from racing and is why the decision is Evo SL / Metaspeed / Vanish. Verify against the organiser's rules, not memory.
- Decide by **Nov 1** whether the Vanish Carbon 3 is being bought. Later than that leaves no room for the three-long-run rule.

## Known disruptions

None known as of Sep 26. The plan does not pre-bend around anything. If a weekend goes, the Friday long moves to Thursday or Saturday and the daughter run flexes; Tuesday stays Tuesday.

## What I am watching for

1. **Shins.** Any anterior or medial shin symptom from week 3 on. Response order: shoe check first (what has the last week's volume been run in?), then volume, then rest. Last year's build broke here in early November.
2. **Resting HR and HR-at-pace relative to volume**, read the way the preferences file says: rising HR in a rising-volume week is adaptation lag, not illness. Rising HR in a *stable* week with poor sleep is the signal.
3. **The Friday closing segment.** Pace at HR 145–150 should trend down across weeks 2–6. Flat or rising after week 4 says the long is being run too hard in the easy part, or the shoe is wrong.
4. **Strength actually happening.** If Tuesday heavy has not happened by week 3, drop it from the plan rather than carrying it as a lie; keep Thursday maintenance.
5. **Gut on the rehearsals.** Any rung-2 signal on a training long at 90 g/h means the race card starts at 75 g/h.

### Correcting the 2025 heart rate

Two reasons the raw 147.9 understates last year:

1. **Wrist optical.** Sep 2025 predates the Coros armband (`hr_data.wrist_optical_until = 2025-09-19`). The 2025 trace is the laggy, under-reading source; today's is armband-grade — zero dropouts, min HR 139 after the warm-up, no sawtooth. Cf. `project_hr_monitor_wrist_unreliable`.
2. **A 13-minute dropout.** Km 8.2–10.9 of the 2025 run records **HR 127 while running 6:13/km**, immediately after a 169 s stop, with valid neighbours at 148.5 (km 5.0–8.2) and 152.9 (km 10.9–14.0). Excluding that window lifts the 2025 average to **150.9**.

So the honest gap is **~+5 bpm, not +8** — and that is still an upper bound, because the device itself under-reads.

### Durability

- **Pa:HR decoupling:** −3.9 % vs −10.2 %. The 2025 figure is partly inflated by a much easier start (HR 138.7 in the first half) and by the stops, so don't read the ratio literally — but the direction survives every correction.
- **Negative split both years**, deeper today: second half 6 s/km faster, and the closing 3.1 km was the *fastest section of the whole run* (5:14/km at HR 161).

### On "förlorade spänsten mot slutet"

The clock disagrees, and the distinction is worth keeping. The last 3.1 km was the fastest segment of the run at a heart rate no higher than km 15–20. What went was **neuromuscular spring** — the eccentric pop on the descents and the short punchy climbs — not aerobic capacity. That is the first quality to go with four weeks of near-zero running and among the fastest to return, and it is specifically what the downhill-eccentric load of this session was prescribed to rebuild. Last year's note on the same loop, for reference: *"lyckades springa alla backar men var rejält trött på slutet"* — the fatigue was there too, and slower.

## Verdict on fitness

Against the athlete who ran 2:24:07 at Lidingöloppet 15 days later: **the aerobic engine at the back end of a two-hour trail run is equal or slightly better, and the front end is 5–10 bpm hot.** That asymmetry is precisely a volume deficit and nothing else — it is the reversible half of the picture, and Sep 12–20 (~70 km) plus a taper is the treatment already on the books.

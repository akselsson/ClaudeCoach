## The shape test (Aug 28, D+13)

Characterized `vo2_or_race` / hard long run. TRIMP 274 — ~4× the next-biggest post-race session, by far the largest stimulus since the race itself.

- **km 1–14 "mellanmjölk":** 5:19/km avg — but with ~+15 bpm drift at constant pace (km 6: ~5:13 @ 140 → km 12–14: same pace @ 160–164).
- **km 15–20 "fartökning":** 4:37/km avg (4:28–4:48) at HR 170→174 — top-of-threshold/vo2-boundary for 6 km.
- **km 21 "tog slut":** unambiguous bonk — pace back to ~5:15 while HR held 172–174.
- 45.7% of the run at HR ≥160. HR trace clean (no wrist-dropout signature); trust the numbers.

## Interpretation

1. **Year-over-year: near-parity after a much bigger race.** The 2025 comparator (id 15625608259, 2025-08-29) was also exactly D+13 — 20.6 km @ 5:00/km, HR 158, also blew up ("helt slut på slutet"). This year: 21.5 km @ 5:07/km, HR 156, more elevation, after a race of 1.75× the distance and 3.3× the TRIMP. Last year that "failed" test preceded a 2:24:07 Lidingöloppet four weeks later. Patrik's in-the-moment pessimism ("inte så troligt att jag förbättrar mig på LL i år") is not supported — if anything the test is the better of the two, adjusted for the hole.
2. **Recovery is incomplete, exactly as the race review predicted.** The +15 bpm easy-pace drift is the "elevated easy-run HR in weeks 2–3 = information" signature. Today measured shape *through* that filter; current 4:37/km-costs-HR-170 is not a fitness statement.
3. **The engine tolerates quality already** (Aug 27: threshold-floor on-kms with zero fade at D+12), consistent with the race review's "the engine was never the limiter."
4. **Cost side:** two consecutive quality days at D+12/13 on ~15 km of intermediate volume, with the right hip flexor only recently off watch. The deviation delivered real information but also a real bill — the next several days must absorb it.

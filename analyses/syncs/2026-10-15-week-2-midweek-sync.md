## Consequences for Fri Oct 16 (and the two Fridays after)

The session stands: **30 km, 25 km Z2 (140–150) from km 1, last 30 min at HR 145–150.** Two amendments to how it's run.

**1. Closing segment at Sollentunavallen. Yes, and make it the standing venue.** Patrik asked whether to run the last 30 min on the track to simulate PRT (a ~350 m flat indoor loop). Reasons it is the right call:

- The segment exists to produce one number, pace at HR 145–150, and to trend it across Oct 16 / 23 / 30 and then the peak-block 30 km gate (≤5:25/km at HR ≤148, season plan). On gravel/mud that number is noisy with terrain and ground condition; on the track it isn't. Lap distance is exact, GPS doesn't matter, the surface is the same every week.
- A 400 m loop is the closest available rehearsal of the race format: flat, one direction, same corner every 90 s, nothing to look at. From week 3 (60 g/h fuelling) the bottle goes at the start/finish line and gets picked up every 2–3 laps, which is the own-table-per-lap rhythm of PRT.
- **The caveat, written into the plan rather than argued away: track pace is not road pace.** Patrik runs faster at Sollentunavallen than on flat tarmac and much faster than on his gravel route. So the Oct 16 track number is **its own baseline**, comparable to Oct 23 and Oct 30 on the same track, **not** to last year's 5:33–5:41 at HR 149–152 on the PRT floor, and the ≤5:25 gate must be read with that in mind (same venue, and a surface offset applied at the Nov 20 race plan). Log the venue in every description.

Practical:
- Route the 25 km Z2 to finish at the track. Lane 1, one direction, auto-lap 1 km is fine.
- **HR 145–150, hard ceiling 152.** The bouncy surface will make a faster pace feel free; the segment is by HR, pace is the output. Don't chase a number.
- Shoe: Superblast for the whole run, or Metaspeed for the segment if a pair can be stashed trackside. The three-long-run race-shoe rule starts counting whenever the Metaspeed first does a segment.
- Description: pace at 145–150, HR, legs, venue.

**2. The first 25 km must be Z2, not easy-then-see.** Last Friday ran 137 avg. The segment is a pace-at-effort read only if it comes at the end of 25 km in the band; 25 km under the band followed by 30 min in it is a fresh-legs tempo, which is a different number.

Thursday's TRIMP 140 the day before isn't ideal, but it was 4 bpm softer than last week and Friday's long then ran fine. Treat it as a known confound, not a reason to shorten.

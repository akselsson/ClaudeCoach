## Thu Jul 30 — the 70 km that became 50.5

**The engine never faded. That is the headline, and it is not a consolation prize — it was the actual question the session existed to answer.**

- **Cardiac drift 0.05 bpm/km — essentially zero across 6+ hours.** First half 139.0 bpm → second half 142.2 bpm (+3.2) while pace went 5:46 → 6:27/km (+41 s/km). The decoupling is entirely on the pace side.
- **Max HR 161 — 87% of max, never near redline in six hours.**
- **The last 10 km, HR *fell* to 137.9** at 7:24/km with three walking kilometres. HR couldn't be driven up because he'd stopped driving it. That is a volitional stop, not a cardiovascular one.
- **Diagnostic nuance worth recording:** genuine dehydration strain normally *raises* HR at a given pace. Here HR stayed flat while pace collapsed. Read: **heat + fluid deficit + muscular/GI shutdown, not aerobic overreach.**

**Pace profile — clean positive split breaking at ~km 31:** 5:34 → 5:53 → 6:08 → **7:17** → 7:24 per 10 km block. A steady mild bleed for 30 km, then a step-change at km 31–36 (worst km 10:34) on the trail section, a brief rally km 40–46, then terminal walking.

**Stoppage tells the hydration story better than anything else: ~49 minutes across 4 escalating stops** — none in the first 14 km, then km 15 (3 min), km 17 (13.7 min), km 32 (16.9 min), km 41 (12.9 min). Classic water-hunting: once fluid went negative, a long stop every ~12–15 km. The 17-minute stop at km 32 sits exactly at the pace break.

**Zones:** easy 45.9% · steady 43.7% · sub-threshold 10.0% (38 min) · threshold 0.4%. The 38 min above 150 is concentrated km 25–46 — which corroborates his own note, *"körde för hårt på stigpartierna."*

**Load:** TRIMP 611 vs 486 on the Jul 22 52 km — **26% more cost for 1.7 km less distance.** The price was time-at-140+ and heat, not kilometres.

**Evo SL Silver over 50 km of mixed road/trail: no shin signal reported.** The shin double-read stays GREEN and gets a third data point.

### The hydration finding — this is a plan-execution error, not a plan error

His own lesson from the description: *"tre skopor umara i 450 ml per timme ger inte tillräckligt med vätska när det är varmt."* True — but the sharper version is that **this is not what `races/2026-07-07-mauerweglauf-energiplan.md` says to do.**

The energiplan's serving rule is: **1× 90 g portion emptied across BOTH 450 ml softflasks — ~45 g per flask, ~10% mix, one refill per ~900 ml.** What he ran on Thursday was ~3 scoops into a *single* 450 ml flask per hour: roughly **double the carb concentration in roughly two-thirds the fluid.** And the hot-weather protocol (+35% fluid, ~675 ml/h, plain-water flask, drink every 10 min, douse head/neck) was not in play at all.

So the correct conclusion is the encouraging one: **the race plan's numbers were never tested and are not implicated.** A hyper-concentrated mix in insufficient fluid on a hot day producing GI shutdown and a water hunt is exactly what that plan is written to prevent. The fix is execution discipline, not a new plan. Worth one deliberate rehearsal of the *actual* serving rule before Berlin.

## Fri Jul 31 — the continuation (19.6 km)

**A clean, unremarkable Day-2 — and reassuring precisely because it is unremarkable.**

- **No fatigue signature.** First half 129.7 bpm @ 5:43/km → second half 139.8 bpm @ 5:29/km. **Negative split.** HR slope 1.1 bpm/km, under the 1.5 line, and the HR rise is bought with pace (pace +4%, HR +8%), not drifted.
- **The comparison that matters:** he ran today *faster than yesterday's second half* (5:29 vs 6:27/km) at a *lower* HR (134.7 vs 140.1 avg). That is the opposite of the suppressed, flattened-HR signature of a body that hasn't recovered.
- **Zones:** easy 72.2% · steady 27.4% · sub-threshold 0.5% · threshold 0%. Genuinely easy.
- **Note:** this Day-2 ran livelier than the last one — Jul 23's 30 km came out `recovery` at 91% easy; this was 72/27 with a 5:29/km close. Shorter, but at higher relative intensity on comparably pre-fatigued legs.
- **Caveat:** max 151 with zero time above 150.5 — no ceiling was tested, so this doesn't rule out a reduced top end. Nothing in the build needs the top end.

**Nothing in the data shows him running out of legs.** The cut reads as a decision (social plans + not wanting to push mildly unhappy legs), which is the correct decision, and the numbers back it as a low-cost one.

## Niggles — the one genuinely new signal

Reported at this sync, and the reason this file is worth reading twice:

- **Left hip flexor** — first noted Jul 26, **still present.** No longer a one-off.
- **Lateral left hip/thigh** — **new.** TFL / glute-med / upper-ITB territory. Came and went during Thursday's run; present now.
- **Quads** — sore at rest. **Benign**: this is ordinary 24–48 h post-50 km soreness and it is exactly what a 50 km with 527 m of vert and a walking finish produces.
- **Under-knee pain** — intermittent during Thursday's run, not present at rest now.
- **Systemically still a bit flat** since Thursday — energy/sleep not fully back.

**The read: stop tracking these as four separate niggles and start tracking one thing — a left hip complex.** An anterior signal (flexor) and a lateral signal (TFL/glute-med) on the *same side*, persisting across days, is a coherent pattern rather than noise: the classic picture is a left hip that is working harder than it should and recruiting neighbours to do it. The intermittent under-knee pain on the same leg fits the same chain (lateral hip → ITB → lateral knee).

None of it is severe — "awareness, alters running a bit," nothing that aborted a session. But **it is the first multi-day, multi-site, same-side signal of the entire build**, and it arrives 15 days before a 161 km race. That combination is what makes it the top watch item, not its current intensity.

## Forward plan — deliberately unchanged

**The 40 km shortfall is not being made up, and the Aug 3–9 taper stands exactly as written in `schedule/2026-07-26-weekly-review.md`.** Reasoning, on record:

1. **A missed stimulus 15 days out cannot be banked.** Adaptation to a big muscular-durability weekend takes ~10–14 days. Anything run this weekend to "recover" the volume would land as fatigue on race day, not fitness.
2. **The thing the weekend was testing already passed.** Cardiac durability over 6+ hours with zero drift, on a hot day, under-fuelled and under-hydrated, on a 4098 kcal session — that is a *harder* durability read than a well-executed 70 km would have given. The engine is not the question.
3. **The taper absorbs the shortfall in the right direction.** Arriving at Berlin 40 km under-loaded and slightly fresher is a materially better error than arriving on schedule and cooked. For a 161 km race, under-trained beats over-trained.
4. **The left hip complex argues the same way.** A new same-side multi-site signal is a reason to take the taper as written or gentler, never to add load.

So: **no re-prescription, no viz regeneration from this file** — it records what happened and leaves the forward plan intact.

Two things I'd fold into the next review rather than decide here:

- **The Aug 7 long run (18–22 km) should be gated on the left hip**, the way Aug 1 was gated on the flexor. If the lateral hip is still talking, that run shrinks or drops — it has almost no fitness value left at that point and real downside.
- **One deliberate hydration rehearsal at the energiplan's actual serving rule** (90 g across ~900 ml, +35% fluid in heat, drink every 10 min) before Berlin, ideally on the Aug 6 or Aug 7 session. Thursday tested a protocol that isn't the plan; the plan itself is still unrehearsed in heat.

## Watch items

1. **The left hip complex (flexor + lateral, same side, persisting).** Top item. Daily check through the taper. Escalation — pain that alters gait, or lateral knee joining in consistently — is a reason to cut the taper's long run entirely.
2. **Systemic flatness.** Still not back 24 h after a 4098 kcal heat effort. Normal at this remove; if it persists past ~Aug 3, it stops being normal and starts being a taper-depth question.
3. **Hydration execution**, per above — the fix is behavioural and cheap, but it needs one rehearsal to count.
4. **Quads** — expected to be quiet within 2–3 days. If they're still sore Aug 3, that's a slower-than-usual bounce and worth noting against the Jul 22→23 precedent, where they recovered overnight.
5. **Shins: still silent** on the Evo SL across a third ultra-distance outing. Nothing to carry.

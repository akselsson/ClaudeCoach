Zones: **89.8 % below 140**, 9.0 % at 140–150, 1.2 % at 150–160, zero above. The 10 % above easy is entirely the stride peaks and their first seconds of recovery.

Load: **TRIMP 25.3 / Edwards 22.6 / suffer 6.** Thursday was 83.5 / 81.4 / 35. A normal 50–60 min easy run in this block is roughly three times this. Nothing here costs Saturday anything.

Three things worth noting about the strides themselves:

- **They were strides, not surges.** Cadence jumped from 171 to 180–182, peak speed 5.3–5.5 m/s on all four within a narrow band, stride 3 the fastest. That is the neuromuscular pop the prescription was for.
- **The walk recoveries were the right call.** HR dropped ~55 bpm inside each 60 s walk. Some of that is optical under-read at walking speed, but it was consistent across all three.
- **Lap HR averages of 107 and 105 on strides 3 and 4 are HR lag, not soft efforts.** HR was 93–95 at the start of each and a 20 s burst is too short to move it; the true peaks (147–148) landed in the following lap. Pace on those laps says the same thing as the others.

Streams are continuous at 1 Hz with no GPS gaps. HR took ~40 s to settle from a 69 start, which is normal start lag and the only artefact in the file.

## Observations

1. **Volume and shoes, the two things the block is actually built on, were right.** 64.6 km on target, every long or medium-long run in a rotation shoe, Tuesday strength done. No shin signal visible, but no description to confirm it either.

2. **Thursday and Friday swapped intensities.** The week's Z2 time landed on a 12.75 km Thursday instead of the 24 km Friday. For this block that is the wrong way round: the whole point of the Friday run is time at 140–150 over long duration, because the Oct 16 / Oct 23 / Oct 30 closing segments at 145–150 are measured against it. Whether Friday ran easy because Thursday was heavy, because of the weather, or by choice, there is no note to say.

3. **The warm bias is the same one as every week, and it's small this week.** Thursday ~6 bpm over, Sunday ~6–10 bpm over, Tuesday reps ~5–7 bpm over. None of it is a Sep 17-style blow-up, and the total load (Thu 124 + Fri 222 + Sun 93) is what a 65 km week should cost. The block file's own guidepost already says it: easy is 130–140, "a target, not a ceiling to push". He runs above prescription by habit (see the Oct 3 sync and the memory on under-prescribing); the fix is not to prescribe less, it's to keep the intensity on the days where it buys something.

4. **Friday's negative split is the fitness signal of the week.** 5:33/km back half at HR 138 with no decoupling, two weeks after a 30 km race. That's aerobically healthy. It still says nothing about pace at 145–150, which remains the Oct 16 number.

5. **Don't trust Strava elevation on wet days.** Friday's 647 m and the week's 1062 m are mostly noise. Future reviews that cite vert should sanity-check against the altitude-stream range before drawing terrain conclusions.

6. **Empty descriptions all week.** The Oct 3 sync asked for HR and leg notes; the Monday sixth-day rule in week 3 depends on "shins silent through week 2", which can't be verified without a note.

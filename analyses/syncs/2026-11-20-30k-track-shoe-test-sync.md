## Sweat rate and fuelling

Patrik weighed nude both times. There were no toilet stops during the run, and he peed once after.

- **~550 ml/h is a lower bound.** The cold kept sweat down and probably raised urine output (cold diuresis). The PRT hall is ~15 °C and dry, so race-day sweat will be higher. 650–750 ml/h is plausible but not measured.
- **Over 9 h, gaining weight is the hyponatremia warning sign.** Today showed 730 ml/h is more than he needs in the cold. In the race, fluid should follow thirst and the conditions, not the carb target.
- **The race card needs carbs and fluid uncoupled.** Today's 30 g per 250 ml mix ties 90 g/h to 750 ml/h. The race plan should get part of the 90 g from a stronger mix or from gels/solids, so that fluid can drop to ~500–600 ml/h without losing carbs.
- **Gut:** clean the whole way at ~87 g/h.
- **Cola:** not taken ("didn't need it"). It is now untested in two rehearsals. The race plan decides: either trial it on the Nov 27 18 km, or keep it optional for late in the race only.

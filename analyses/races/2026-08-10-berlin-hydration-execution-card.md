# Berlin Mauerweglauf — hydration & cooling execution card

**Race:** Sat 2026-08-15, start 06:00, 162.1 km, 26 VPs, 3 drop bags (km 33 / km 70 / km 102).
**Forecast:** 32–36 °C, sunny, **dry** (RH 13–35%). Sunrise 05:49, sunset 20:32.

This is the operating document. It supersedes the hydration sections of `races/2026-07-07-mauerweglauf-energiplan.md` and consolidates `races/2026-08-10-berlin-heat-hydration-update.md`, which remains the place to look for *why*. The energiplan's non-hydration content (course facts, caffeine architecture, race-day hip protocol via `schedule/2026-08-07-weekly-review.md`) is unchanged and still applies.

**The three rules that matter most, if everything else falls out of your head:**

1. **Don't bank time in the cool morning.** Every minute gained before 09:30 is repaid with interest at 15:30.
2. **Weak mix, lots of it.** Half-portions once it's over 30 °C. Carbs come from gels and cola instead.
3. **Water on the outside at every single VP.** Twenty-six water points beat three thermoses.

## The operating table

Anchored to the energiplan's 19:52 scaffold. **If you are an hour down on it, shift each row an hour later** — the two hot rows barely move, the night rows arrive cooler. That is one more reason not to chase the scaffold.

| | **A** | **B** | **C — the crux** | **D** | **E** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **km** | 0–33 | 33–70 | **70–102** | 102–128 | 128–162 |
| **Clock** | 06:00–09:30 | 09:30–13:45 | **13:45–17:45** | 17:45–21:00 | 21:00–01:52 |
| **Temp** | 20→26 °C | 26→35 °C | **35–37 °C** | 36→32 °C | 32→25 °C |
| **Drink** | 550 ml/h | 800 ml/h | **950 ml/h** | 800 ml/h | 600 ml/h |
| **Mix** | **FULL** 90 g / 900 ml | **HALF** 45 g / 900 ml | **HALF** | **HALF** | **HALF** |
| **Carbs** | ~88 g/h — mix + 1 gel/h | ~78 g/h — mix + 1 gel/h + cola | ~68 g/h — mix + cola, gels as tolerated | ~65 g/h — mix + cola + gel | ~60 g/h — mix + cola + gel |
| **Salt** | mix only; first U Hydrate ~km 25 | **1 U Hydrate/h** | **1 U Hydrate/h** (+FastChews if cramping) | **1 U Hydrate/h** | 1 U Hydrate/90 min |
| **Cooling** | from ~km 20: douse at VPs | douse + rewet **every VP** | **maximum** — douse + rewet + ice | douse + rewet | taper off after ~23:00 |
| **Carry** | 3 flasks (2 mix, 1 water) | 3 flasks | **3–4 flasks** | 3 flasks | 2–3 flasks |
| **Segment total** | ~1.9 L | ~3.4 L | **~3.8 L** | ~2.6 L | ~2.9 L |

**Total drunk ≈ 14.5 L.** Plus roughly **8–10 L poured over yourself**, sourced entirely at VPs — that water is not part of the drinking budget, ask for it separately.

**Switch to half-mix at the km 33 bag**, even though it's only 26 °C there. It crosses 30 °C around km 45 and there is no bag between — switching early costs a little carbohydrate and saves you making a decision at 30 °C with a flask in each hand.

**950 ml/h is the ceiling of what a gut absorbs while running.** Sweat rate in block C will exceed that. **A fluid deficit is unavoidable — accept it.** The way to shrink it is to run slower and cool the skin, not to drink more.

## The VP routine

Twenty-six stations. Same six things, every time, sixty seconds:

1. **Fill** — mix flask and water flask both.
2. **Douse** — head, neck, forearms, shirt. **Never down the legs** — water in the shoes over 160 km is a blister problem.
3. **Rewet** — bandana, cap, sleeves.
4. **Salt** — U Hydrate into the water flask, on the hour.
5. **Posture** — stand tall, hips forward, reset. *(The hip protocol's check-in — same cadence, same stop.)*
6. **Cola** — take a cup from km 50 on. It's the coldest thing on the table and it's carbohydrate you don't have to carry or mix.

### Bag 3 — km 102, VP17 Sportplatz Teltow · ~17:45 · ~36 °C

**This is the last bag. 60 km and roughly 8 hours come out of it.**

- **THERMOS — plan it as cold water. Ice is a bonus.**
- **NIGHT GEAR: headlamp + spare batteries + reflective vest** *(mandatory 21:00–06:00, on by VP21)*
- 4–5 × 45 g U Sport
- 6 gels · 2 bars
- 5 × U Hydrate · FastChews

- Optional: Altra Torin 8 + dry socks
- **No warm layer needed** — it's still 25 °C at the finish

**Powder total ≈ 825 g** (2 × 90 g + ~15 × 45 g), against the energiplan's ~1030 g. The heat plan carries **less powder, more salt, more gels, more cola.** Pack accordingly — mostly 45 g ziplocks, not 90 g pouches.

**Redundancy note:** you pass each bag exactly once, so "spare" means carrying it out, not leaving it behind. The U Hydrate counts above (19 total against ~13 needed) are deliberate.

## Caffeine

Unchanged in architecture, shifted in timing: **first dose at the km 102 bag (~17:45)**, once the temperature has started falling — not at km 96 as originally written, which now lands inside the hottest hours.

- **km 102:** 100 mg tablet
- **through the night:** 30–100 mg per depot, tablets not gels, so late g/h stays low
- **km ~148:** U Intend 250 mg as the final one-shot

## Decision gates

| Where | When | What you are deciding |
|---|---|---|
| **km 45** | ~11:00 | **De-escalation check.** The hot protocol is already running. If it is clearly not going above 30 °C, step back toward the normal fluid column. *(This is the inverse of the energiplan's original trigger.)* |
| **km 70** | ~13:45 | **The real decision point of the race.** Halfway, 8 hours in, 35 °C. Hip and posture, GI, urine colour, whether you are cooling successfully. Take the time — it is a bag stop anyway. |
| **km 102** | ~17:45 | Night gear on, first caffeine, second formal reassessment. Last resupply — check you are carrying enough for 60 km. |

**Urine check at each bag.** Pale straw is the target. Dark and scant means behind on fluid. Clear, copious *and* nauseous means behind on **salt** — the answer is a tablet, not another bottle.

## Red flags

### Heat illness — stop, shade, ice, cold fluid. Do not restart until it clears.

- **Any CNS symptom: confusion, disorientation, unusual irritability, stumbling, difficulty following a conversation.** ← **this is the discriminator.** Ordinary hour-12 fatigue does not touch cognition; heat illness does, before you notice.
- Sweating stops, or chills/goosebumps while it is still hot
- Headache with nausea and no other explanation
- No urination for 4+ hours, or very dark urine

**If anyone at a VP asks whether you're alright, treat it as data, not fussing.** Twenty minutes in shade is recoverable.

### Hyponatremia — more salt, less water

Sloshing stomach · puffy fingers · ring or watch strap tightening · nausea with clear copious urine.

### Neuro / back — DNF criteria, unchanged

Numbness or pins-and-needles down the leg · loss of strength (knee buckling, foot slap or drag) · symptoms going bilateral · any bowel or bladder change. *Per `schedule/2026-08-07-weekly-review.md`. Ordinary aching and the familiar hip/knee grumble are not on this list.*

**DNF over injury, every time.** Lidingöloppet is Sep 26 and a disc is a multi-season problem.

## If the gut goes quiet

The Jul 30 failure mode, one degree closer in heat because blood is diverted away from the gut exactly when intake must rise. Diluting the mix is the primary defence and is why this plan exists. If it happens anyway:

**Stop the carbs for 30–45 minutes. Plain water, salt and cola only, down to ~30 g/h. Then rebuild slowly.**

*Lower g/h briefly beats shutting the gut down.*

## Before the gun

**Mon 10 – Thu 13 Aug — heat acclimation, passive only.** Sauna or 40 °C bath. Mon 25–30 min standalone · **Tue immediately after the 8 km** (highest yield) · Wed 25–30 min standalone · **Thu after the shakeout**, 20–25 min. **Fri: none** — travel and sleep. Rehydrate fully after each. No overdressed running; the back doesn't need the mechanical load.

**Thu–Fri:** normal fluids, generous salt on food. **No glycerol or sodium-hyperhydration protocols** — untested, and race week is not for experiments.

**Fri:** freeze the thermoses. Pre-chill each one first — fill with ice water for 30 min, dump, then pack with **large cubes**, tight, to the brim, no added water. Wide-mouth flasks only: a narrow neck holding a block you can't extract is the classic failure. Wrap each in a foil emergency blanket and pack clothing around it.

**Sat 04:30–06:00:** ~500 ml with electrolyte 90 min before the start, then sip. Sunscreen on. Bandana wetted. Ask whether the VPs have ice — if they do, the thermoses become redundancy rather than the plan, which is a much better position.

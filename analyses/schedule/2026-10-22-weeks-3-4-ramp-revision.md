# Weeks 3–4 revision: longer Friday, steady week instead of down week

Applies on top of `2026-09-26-prt-block-1-sep28-nov1.md` (weeks 3–4), `2026-09-27-block-1-schedule-update.md` and the carry-forward in `../syncs/2026-10-16-friday-30k-track-segment-sync.md`. Where they disagree, this file wins for Oct 23 – Nov 1. Weeks 5–6 are an outline; the Nov 1 review still sets the details.

## Why (changes prior guidance)

The block had week 3 at ~85 km with a 32 km long, then a down week at ~70 km, then a peak at 95–110. Three points from Patrik on Oct 22, all checked against Strava:

1. **Week 3 wasn't going to reach 85.** Monday was dropped after the Oct 16 Evo SL test, and Tuesday's Kundo 4×4 was only 6.8 km. Mon–Thu = 22.2 km, so the five-day grid lands ~72.
2. **A big jump is riskier than a steady ramp.** Going from ~70 to 95–110 is +35–55%. Last year's shins broke on a 79 → 101 km step (Oct 6 – Nov 9 2025: 65, 91, 94, 79, 101).
3. **There has been little load to recover from.** Run km since Mauerweglauf: 3, 34, 17, 43, 54, ~34 (Lidingö week), 39, 65, 72. Two build weeks don't warrant a down week, and the taper (weeks 7–8) gives the real recovery before Dec 4.

The 32 km was a cautious number. Oct 16's 31 km ran at HR 135 with no drift, legs and shins fine afterwards, and the week since has been easy. Running past 30 km is the one gap against last year (40 km on the track by Oct 17 2025), so the long run grows faster.

**Shin protection now comes from a symptom rule, not a down week:** any anterior/medial shin signal → drop the extra easy runs first (Saturday's extra run, then Sunday's added km, then the Monday run), keep the Friday long and segment. All easy volume in rotation shoes (Torin / Superblast / Megablast); Evo SL and Metaspeed for segments only.

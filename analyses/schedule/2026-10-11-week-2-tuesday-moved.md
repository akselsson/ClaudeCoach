# Week 2 schedule update — Tuesday session moved to Monday

Applies on top of `2026-09-26-prt-block-1-sep28-nov1.md` week 2 and `2026-09-27-block-1-schedule-update.md`. Where they disagree, this file wins for Oct 12–18 only.

## Why

Patrik can't make the Tuesday lunch interval run on Oct 13 and wants one quality session and one strength session in the week. He can't fit both the run and the lift on Monday, so they split across Monday and Tuesday.

The one fixed point is **Friday Oct 16**: 30 km with the last 30 min at HR 145–150, the block's first pace-at-effort read. Every placement below is chosen so that session is run fresh.

- **Quality on Monday, not Wednesday.** Wednesday is the buffer between the hard day and the Thursday/Friday stack. A Wednesday session leaves only Thursday before the 30 km, and last Thursday ran steady (HR 146) rather than easy. Monday keeps three days of spacing. Sunday Oct 11's 11 km progression was a modest load (TRIMP 93), so a hard Monday is fine.
- **Strength on Tuesday, not Thursday.** Tuesday heavy → Wed off → Thu easy → Fri long is the standing schedule's own spacing; nothing new is risked. A Thursday lift two days before a 30 km is the placement the preferences file says to cut when Friday matters.
- **4×4 at VO2, not a threshold substitute.** I proposed 3 × 8 min at 160–168 (more race-specific minutes per session; the 4×4 has twice ignored a threshold cap). Patrik's view: he will run the Kundo 4×4 as VO2 going forward, and consistency in one repeated format — form work, raising the top end, a readable Sep 15 / Oct 6 / Oct 12 progression — is worth more than one week of threshold. Accepted: the per-week difference is marginal (last week's 4×4 cost TRIMP 77 and showed no knock-on), and the comparison across identical sessions has real value. The standing decision is now in `../preferences/2026-05-03-training-preferences.md` under the Kundo formats.

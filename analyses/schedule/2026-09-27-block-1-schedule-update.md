# Block 1 schedule update — Sep 27

Applies on top of `2026-09-26-prt-block-1-sep28-nov1.md`. Where the two disagree, this file wins. Standing-schedule changes (2) and (3) are also written into `../preferences/2026-05-03-training-preferences.md`.

## 1. Sat Oct 3 — Förbifartspremiären 12 km

Day 7 after a 30 km race at HR 169. The block had "daughter run, Z1, or off" here and the 60–75 min easy run on Sunday.

**Run it by feel, as proposed.** Cruise is the default. The two branches:

| Legs on the day | How to run it | HR |
|---|---|---|
| Heavy, or anything speaking up in quads/calves | Cruise the whole thing, easy | <140 |
| Good | Cruise to ~6 km, then pick up to **100 km effort for the last 6 km** | 145–150, never above 155 |

Why the cap: 145–150 is the band the whole block is built on (the Friday closing segments from week 2). Running the back half of a flat 12 km there gives a first pace-at-effort read a week early, at no recovery cost. Anything above 155 is a second race inside eight days, and the first quality day is Tuesday Oct 6.

Practical:
- If the course is in the tunnel, GPS pace will be nonsense. Run the pick-up on HR and feel, ignore the watch pace, read the effort off the official splits afterwards.
- **Shoe: Superblast or Torin.** 12 km is over the Boston/Evo SL cap.
- Warm-up jog 1–2 km. No strides.
- Log the HR and how the legs felt in the Strava description.

**Sun Oct 4 becomes:** daughter run Z1 if she wants it, otherwise 40–45 min easy <140, or off. Not the 60–75 min that was on this day; the 12 km took that slot.

Week 0 total lands at 35–45 km, inside the recovery-week target.

## 2. Monday commute-home runs (option)

Allowed in **weeks 1, 2 and 3** (Oct 5, 12, 19). Not Sep 28 (D+2, the review said no running) and not Oct 26 (down week: the point is total load dropping).

Rules:
- **Easy, HR <140, no strides.** Kundo is the next evening; the Monday run must cost nothing.
- **Volume shoe** — Torin, Superblast or Megablast. Carry it to work. A 15+ km run home in a Boston or Evo SL is exactly the shin pattern from last year.
- **Counts on top of the block target**, not inside it. Don't trim Thursday or Sunday to compensate. Week targets with a Monday run: ~72–77, ~90, ~100 km (assuming the run route is the same 17 km as the bike commute — if it's shorter, scale down).
- **Week 3 is the watch week.** With a Monday run it lands at ~100 km, which is the volume where last year's shins started. Condition: shins silent through week 2 and every volume run in weeks 2–3 in the rotation shoes. Any shin signal in week 3 → skip the Monday run that week. Thursday and the Friday long stay.

## 3. Filling the second off day

Yes. **Monday is the fill day; Wednesday stays off.** Reasoning: Wednesday is the buffer between Tuesday quality + heavy strength and the Thursday/Friday volume stack. Monday already has the commute-run option, and a sixth easy day the evening before Kundo costs less than one wedged between the two stress days.

- **With a commute run:** that is the fill.
- **Without one:** Monday evening 8–10 km easy, HR <140, volume shoe. Same counting rule as above.
- **Not in week 0 (recovery) or week 4 (down week).** Six run days in weeks 1–3 only. The preference file already allowed a sixth day in peak weeks; this extends it to build weeks.
- Wednesday bike commute remains allowed as before; it is cross-training, not the fill.

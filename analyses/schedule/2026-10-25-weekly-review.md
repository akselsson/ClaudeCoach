That matches last year's race: 145 avg over 12 h, and the 149–152 stretch (km 31–60) was the one he flagged as "för hög puls i början relativt farten". **Decision: the race-effort segment band becomes HR 142–148, hard ceiling 150.** The segment is still run by feel at "what I would hold for 100 km" with HR as the check, and pace remains the output. The sub-9 gate (≤5:25 road at HR ≤148) is unchanged; its ceiling already sits inside the new band. 60 g/h from the start worked: the stomach was upset *before* the run (one toilet stop, otherwise fine), not from the mix. 90 g/h on Oct 30 is the real test.
- **Strength: zero sessions in week 3.** Social activities, not avoidance; done in weeks 1 and 2. The season plan's "drop Tuesday heavy if it hasn't stuck by week 3" rule does not fire. **Decision: both sessions stay.** Tuesday heavy is the one that matters; Thursday maintenance becomes optional in peak weeks, dropped first when time is short.
- **Thursday: steady without strides, three for three.** Patrik's own account: he simply forgets the strides and wants to remember them. **Decision:** Thursday is written as **medium-long 14–16 km at HR ≤145** (what it has actually been, TRIMP ~125–140, which Friday has tolerated every week). - **Sun Oct 18 at HR 141 on trail vs <135.** Hilly Lone Peak run; km 4–7 at 144–149. Minor, no consequence seen. Sunday stays <135 on the flat, and on trail the instruction is "walk the climbs".
- **Sat Oct 24: 12 km with the daughter, no solo add-on, gear unset.** Her run has grown to 12 km at ~6:10/km; fine as the whole of Saturday. Shoe was the Superblast. Set gear on every run; the sync files read it.
- **Race shoe.** The Vanish Carbon 3 is bought, keep/return undecided, and Patrik will also try the ASICS Metaspeed Edge Tokyo before deciding. The season plan's rule (three Friday segments before Nov 13 in any shoe that starts Dec 4) now has exactly three Fridays left: Oct 30, Nov 6, Nov 13.

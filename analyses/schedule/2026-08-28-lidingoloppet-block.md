# Lidingöloppet block — Aug 29 → Sep 26

## Decision of record

**Lidingöloppet 2026 (Sat Sep 26, 30 km) is raced with real effort.** Confirmed by Patrik 2026-08-28. This upgrades the season plan's framing ("B-race, don't try to PR, enjoy a fast 30k off ultra fitness") — a deliberate change, made with the A-race done and nothing left in the season to protect.

- **A-goal: sub-2:20** (Patrik's earlier-season wish; ≈ 4:36/km on the 2025-measured 30.44 km, ~3% faster than last year).
- **B-goal: beat 2:24:07** (LL 2025, 4:44/km, avg HR 168).
- Which goal gets raced is decided by the **Sep 18 decision workout** (below), not by feelings on the start line.

**Feasibility, honestly:** sub-2:20 asks for ~8 s/km more than a 2025 race that already averaged HR 168. Against that: the 2026 engine is bigger (a 100-mile build at HR-cap 128 vs a 90k relay leg), the D+13 shape test landed at near-parity with 2025's despite 3.3× the race load behind it, and the Aug 27 threshold session showed no on-rep fade at D+12. It's a genuine maybe — which is exactly what the decision workout is for.

## Why this supersedes the race review's table

The race review (2026-08-16) prescribed one short tempo from week 4 and one touch in week 5 — written for a "no ambition" LL. Two things changed: (1) the ambition, above; (2) the data — Patrik ran his 2025-style D+13 shape test (see `../syncs/2026-08-28-return-to-running-sync.md`), which showed the engine tolerates quality but autonomic recovery is incomplete (+15 bpm easy-pace drift). The revision: **roughly double the quality, but gate it on recovery signals rather than pulling it earlier.** Weeks 1–2 of the review (already past) stand as written; this file replaces weeks 3–6.

The two watch items carry over unchanged: **right hip flexor** (now doubly relevant after a TRIMP-274 day) and **autonomic recovery** (elevated easy HR = information, not fitness).

## The decision workout (Fri Sep 18)

20–22 km with 12 km continuous at 4:36–4:40/km, 8 days out. The rule:

- Block sits at **HR ≤ ~166** and feels like "could hold this in a race for 30 km" → **race the A-goal**: even effort targeting sub-2:20, using HR ~168 as the ceiling through the hills.
- Block costs **HR 170+** or fades → **race the B-goal**: go out at 2025 effort (HR ~165–168), race the last 10 km, beat 2:24 on the back half.

Either way LL is raced by HR-capped effort — the pacing philosophy the 100-miler just validated — with pace as the output. LL's terrain makes km splits noisy; the on-course reference is last year's own race (4:44/km avg at HR 168).

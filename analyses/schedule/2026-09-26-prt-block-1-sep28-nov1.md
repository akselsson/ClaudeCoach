# Block 1 — Sep 28 to Nov 1 (recovery week + 4 rebuild weeks)

Under `../season-plan/2026-09-26-prt-100k-season-plan.md`. Day grid from `../preferences/2026-05-03-training-preferences.md`.

## Guideposts

- **Easy:** HR <140. Most runs. Recent easy runs have landed 130–140 at 5:15–5:45/km; that is the target, not a ceiling to push.
- **Z2 long:** HR 140–150, steady from the start, not drift-up-from-easy.
- **100 km effort (the closing segment):** **HR 145–150.** Pace is the output. Last year's best 30 km at this HR was ~5:40/km real; expect 5:30–5:45 in week 2 and watch it trend down.
- **Threshold:** 160–170, Kundo only.
- **Strides:** 4 × 20 s, no HR target, on Thursdays.
- **Fuelling on long runs from week 3:** the race mix — diluted Umara at 6–8% plus solid/gel — at 60 g/h in week 3 and 90 g/h in week 4. Note any gut signal in the Strava description.

**Shoe rule for every run:** volume in Torin 8 / Superblast 3 / Megablast, rotated. Boston 13 and Evo SL only on runs ≤10 km. Metaspeed (or another race candidate) on the Friday closing segment from week 2, so the three-long-run rule is met by Nov 13.

## Nov 1 review decides

1. **Peak-block ceiling:** 95–105 or 105–115 for weeks 5–6, on shins, sleep and whether the Friday closing pace has trended down across weeks 2–4.
2. **Vanish Carbon 3:** buy or not. If bought, it runs the weeks 5, 6 and 7 long runs.
3. **Strength:** if Tuesday heavy has not happened in weeks 1–3, drop it from the peak-block file.
4. **Fuelling:** any rung-2 signal on the week-3 or week-4 rehearsals → the race card starts at 75 g/h.

## Reality check

- Week 1 at ~60 km is +10% on the Sep 14 week and about what Sep 7–20 averaged with the illness week excluded. Fine.
- The 25 km week-1 long is below today's 30 km race and above the 23 km long of Sep 11. Growth to 36 km by week 4 is +44% over four weeks, which is steep but on a runner whose peak long this year was 163 km and whose last-year build did 91–101 km weeks in the same calendar slot. The counterweight is the shoe rule.
- The closing segment at HR 145–150 is the same intensity band as last year's fastest race stretch, reintroduced at 30 min, not 90.
- Tuesday and Friday remain the only stress days; Thursday and the weekend are volume.

## Discrepancies and decisions

- **Week at 81.7 vs ~100 km.** **Decision:** no make-up. The gate run was the week, and we're now in taper territory.
- **Sun run skipped (busy).** It was optional in the plan. **Decision:** nothing to change.
- **Sat 16.1 km vs ~12 km, HR spike to 166.** Sensor artifact (no armband). **Decision:** treat it as a Z1 leg. Wear the armband on anything where HR will be read: Tuesday, Friday, and every race-effort segment.
- **Left lateral knee/foot (Thu Nov 12).** It settled during Friday's 44 km and is fine when walking, but it hasn't been tested at quality or at a long run on rested legs. **Decision:** Tuesday and Thursday are the checks before Friday (contingency in the Fri Nov 20 row). Patrik put it down to Monday's sixth-day run, so there are no more Monday runs before the race. Weeks 7 and 8 are taper weeks anyway.
- **Strength.** The Nov 9 rule said strength would be dropped if the Nov 10 session didn't happen. It did happen, five days late, on Sunday. **Decision:** strength stays for one more session on Tue Nov 17, as planned: the last heavy one, with a third fewer sets. No split squats if they provoke the outside of the left knee. After that it's core and mobility only until the race.
- **Race plan: Mon–Wed this week → after Friday.** Patrik would rather write it with the rehearsal data in hand. **Decision:** accepted. Friday rehearses the provisional parameters written in this file: race-start HR, 90 g/h at 750 ml/h, cola in the last hour. The race plan (`races/`) is written Sat Nov 21 – Mon Nov 23 from that run.
- **Sweat test: 60 min on the treadmill → weighing around Friday's 30 km.** Patrik's point is right: the gym is much warmer than the race hall (~15 °C, dry), so it would over-read. Outside it's ~5 °C, so it under-reads. A separate session also costs a run in a taper week. **Decision:** weigh nude before and after the Friday 30 km, and log fluid drunk and any toilet stops. That gives a sweat rate at race intensity with race fuelling. Treat it as a **lower bound** and add a margin for 15 °C in the race plan. The Nov 13 run already showed 750 ml/h sits fine in the gut.
- **Fri Nov 20: track → varied terrain.** Patrik has done two track longs in a row and feels varied terrain stresses the body less. That makes sense with a fresh lateral left knee: 100+ laps of bends is the most repetitive loading in the block. The track's specific value, lap pacing and a table on the loop, has been rehearsed twice (Nov 6, Nov 13). **Decision:** run Friday on **flat-to-rolling road or gravel**, not trail, because the Adios is a road racing shoe. Pace by HR, not by laps. Fuel from a loop past home or the car every ~30 min, or carry it.

## What I'm watching for

- **The left lateral knee/foot.** Tue and Thu are the checks before Friday. It's on the same side as the summer hip/knee complex. **PRT is a ~350 m loop, so the bends come even more often than on the track.** It **starts counter-clockwise and switches to clockwise after 5 h** (Patrik, Nov 16). That's ~55 km with the left leg on the inside of every bend, and the inside leg is the one usually linked to lateral knee load. The race plan needs a rule for what to do if the knee speaks before the 5 h switch; after the switch the load moves to the other side.
- **Friday's closing segment.** First real read of pace at HR 142–148 since Oct 23. Expect it at or faster than ~5:05–5:10 on flat road. Slower than ~5:20 at that HR means the taper starts with the legs flat, not that fitness is missing. The gate already answered that.
- **Gut at 750 ml/h + cola.** Rung 2 or worse on Friday means the race card starts at 75 g/h, per the season plan's rule.
- **Taper restlessness.** Patrik tends to run over prescription. In weeks 7–8 an extra 5 km of easy running is harmless. An unplanned tempo or a partner-pace 15 km at HR 145 the day before Fri Nov 27 is not.
- **Throat and sleep.** Any recurrence of the sore throat in the taper means stop. Nothing left in the build is worth more than arriving healthy.

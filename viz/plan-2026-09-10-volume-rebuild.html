Gate 1 passed.
The limiter was detraining, not illness.

Confirmed 2026-09-10: no fever, no chest symptoms, no niggle, resting HR 46 against a baseline of 44, and 50 minutes to HR 170 tolerated cleanly. The illness chapter is closed. What replaced it as the limiter is visible in one number — four weeks at 2/34/17/10 km, against 96–109 km six weeks earlier. Patrik's own read is the load-bearing insight: a resting HR that never sits at 44 except in a higher-volume period is a training-state readout, not an illness residue, and the treatment is running, not rest.

This corrects a structural error, not just a data point: the Sep 7 file cut volume while leaving the hard sessions (Ursvik, the decision workout) untouched — exactly backwards. Planning rule going forward: when a week has to shrink, the hard session shrinks first; easy volume is the last thing cut. So volume goes up — ~55 km through Sep 13, ~70 km Sep 14–20, ~30 km + race for Sep 21–26 — while intensity comes down. Fri Sep 11's Ursvik loop stays, HR cap raised to ≤155 (from 150) with a 2:30 hard stop (from 2:15), now unconditional since Gate 1 passed. Sat/Sun Sep 12–13 become real easy runs, not walks. Wed Sep 16 becomes an easy evening run instead of off.

Fri Sep 18 is demoted from a go/no-go gate to a race-pace rehearsal. A test eight days out, on a body still mid-rebuild, would systematically under-read race-day fitness and push the goal down for a reason that won't be true on the start line. So: 18–20 km with 6–8 km continuous at goal effort, run by feel, HR ≤~166 — it rehearses fuelling and rhythm and sanity-checks the legs, and it sets no goal and cancels nothing.

The race goal is deliberately undecided. Neither sub-2:20 nor beat-2:24:07 is set in advance — Lidingöloppet is raced by HR-capped effort (HR 165–168), pace as the output, with 2025's own race (4:44/km at HR 168) as the on-course reference. If the rebuilt volume has landed, the pace at that heart rate will show it early and the clock takes care of itself.

Illness stop-rules are retired. Carried forward unchanged: right hip flexor silent as of Sep 10 — drop the next quality slot if it reacts, don't trade it for volume. Max HR stays 184 (the Sep 4 184 was a fever artefact). Race shoe still open — gravel/trail/road mix, decided in race week.

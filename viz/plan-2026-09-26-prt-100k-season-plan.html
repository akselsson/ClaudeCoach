—— BLOCK 01 · WEEK-BY-WEEK ——

Ten weeks, one closing segment.

Lidingöloppet is done — 2:27:15, time lost to a congested start and a surrendered final climb, not a blow-up in between. The next ten weeks rebuild toward PRT 100 km on Dec 4: a recovery week, four weeks taking volume from 60 to 85 km with the Friday long growing to 36 km, a three-week peak at 95–110 km carrying the sub-9 pacing gate, then a two-step taper into race week. The specific work lives entirely inside the Friday long's closing segment — 30 to 90 minutes at HR 145–150, 100 km effort. Everything else stays easy. Shins are the one thing that broke last year's build at this exact point in the calendar; the shoe rotation below exists so that doesn't repeat.

Block 02 — Peak, Taper & Race Week

Nov 2 – Dec 4 · 5 weeks · day-by-day detail drafted at the Nov 1 review
WEEKS 5–6 · NOV 2–15

The peak

95–110 km weeks, ceiling pinned at the Nov 1 review on shins and how week 5 lands. Friday longs 40 km then 45 km, the second a back-to-back with the daughter's Saturday run. The closing segment grows to 60–90 min at HR 145–150.

Week 6's long carries the sub-9 gate: a flat 30 km at HR ≤148 holding ≤5:25/km, Nov 13, in the intended race shoe. Pass → the race plan is written for sub-9 with sub-10 as the net. Miss by >10 s/km → paced for a certain sub-10, with sub-9 only if the day allows.

Watch: shins, Friday closing pace trending down, gut on 90 g/h.
WEEK 7 · NOV 16–22

First taper step

Volume eases from the peak but the anchor doesn't: a 30 km Friday long carries the full dress-rehearsal fuelling card — race mix, cola from hour five, the table set up as on race day. Also the third of the three long runs a race shoe needs before Nov 13.

Feeds: the race-shoe short list, narrowing toward the Nov 13/20 calls.
WEEK 8 · NOV 23–29

Second taper step

45–55 km, long run down to 20 km. Kundo easy. The race plan itself is written around Nov 20, gated on the week-6 test: sub-9 pacing with sub-10 as the net, or a sub-10 pace with sub-9 only if the day allows.

Decides: final race shoe, fuelling-card starting rate (90 vs 75 g/h).
WEEK 9 · NOV 30–DEC 4

Race week

~15 km before the race, which lands on Friday — the normal long-run day. PRT 100 km, ~350 m loop, own table: stretch sub-9:00 (5:24/km), hard floor sub-10:00 (6:00/km), the pacing call locked in by the race plan.

Fuelling: 90 g/h at 6–8% dilution, solids/gel from hour one, cola from ~hour five.

After Dec 4

What comes after the loop.

No B-race follows this one — PRT 100 km closes the 2026 season. Recovery afterward follows the same pattern as every big effort this year: days off, then easy jogging back in, no fixed timeline until the body says otherwise. The next season-plan file starts from scratch once that's clear.

One note is already banked for 2027, not for this build: next summer and autumn should carry more hills and more threshold/tempo/speed, both crowded out by two ultra builds this year. Recorded from the Lidingöloppet debrief, where "till nästa år ska träna mer backe" was the ironic annual ritual — the real version of it is filed here for when the 2027 season plan gets written.

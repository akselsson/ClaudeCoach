—— INTERIM BLOCK · WEEK-BY-WEEK ——

No race. Threshold instead.

PRT 100 km on Dec 4 was canceled on Nov 21, so the taper and race week from the Nov 16 review are void. The aerobic base is at its season high (105 / 82 / 67 km the last three weeks), and Patrik wants threshold work rather than volume — a fit match for the 2027 note about more threshold and speed. This is a 4–6 week interim block, to roughly early January, while the next goal is chosen; when it is, this block becomes either its base phase (a spring ultra) or its opening phase.

Two quality days, no more. Tuesday: Kundo as the group sets it, with heavy strength in the evening (first one Dec 1 at ~⅔ load). Friday: a sub-threshold session in the long-run slot, HR capped at 160–162, every rep at the same pace — 3×10 min on Nov 27, 4×8 min on Dec 4, then 2×15, 3×12, 2×20. Everything else is easy, HR <140, ≥50–60 km a week: Patrik’s HR drifts up below ~70 km, so cut hard work first and never the easy kilometres. Monday stays off two more weeks for the left lateral knee/foot; if it speaks in Friday’s faster reps, drop Friday first.
